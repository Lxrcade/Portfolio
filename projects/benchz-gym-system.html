Benchz Gym Management System

A full-stack gym management platform built with Laravel 12, Livewire, and Tailwind CSS. Admins manage members, plans, billing, and attendance through a role-based portal with email OTP verification; members get their own portal for invoices, plans, and check-ins.

Highlights

Highlights

Highlights

  • Retention engine automated membership expiry detection, at-risk/lapsed member scoring, and a win-back campaign system with coupon minting, queued email delivery, and audit logging

  • Payments - PayMongo checkout with QR Ph / GCash / Maya, webhook handling, and transaction records

  • Scan terminal - real-time QR attendance check-in with busy-state handling

  • Reports & dashboards - KPI dashboards, PDF exports, and activity logs

Tools & Languages used

Tools & Languages used

Tools & Languages used

  • Tailwind CSS & Vanilla CSS

  • Alpine.js

  • Livewire Volt

  • PHP Laravel
